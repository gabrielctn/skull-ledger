Skull Ledger is an independently developed utility for recording scores while users play a physical card game. It does not provide a playable digital version of that game.

Changes addressing Guideline 4.1(a):
- Renamed the app to Skull Ledger, including the store listing, device display name and in-app branding.
- Replaced the crowned-skull app icon and launch artwork with an original score-ledger illustration.
- Removed the crowned skull, mermaid, parrot and skull-decorated treasure chest illustrations from the app. The app now uses ledger, compass and trophy illustrations.
- Removed every third-party game and publisher name from the English and French descriptions, the screenshot captions, the support and privacy pages, the app's home screen, the shared game recap and the feedback email.
- Replaced the store screenshots with captures from the revised app.

No sign-up is required. To test offline scorekeeping, tap New game, enter player names and start. Shared tables and live following need an internet connection.

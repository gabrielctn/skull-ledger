Keep the scores. Enjoy the game.

Skull Ledger keeps bids, tricks and bonus points in one place, calculates each round, and saves your crew's game history.

• Score every round. Enter bids, tricks won and bonuses, then see the updated standings immediately.

• Share a table with your crew. Invite friends with a link or QR code. Anyone at the shared table can keep score from their own phone, and finished games join the same history, statistics and leaderboard.

• Follow scores live. Other players can open a read-only view of the standings and round details on their phone while you keep score.

• Choose your scoring options. Configure scoring variants, special-card bonuses, two-player play and the round structure to match your physical game.

• Keep your crew's history. Explore score charts, win rates, exact bids, podiums, streaks, records and game duration.

• Pick up where you left off. Resume an autosaved game, correct an earlier round, start a rematch and share the final recap.

• Keep score offline. Shared tables, cloud backup and live following require an internet connection.

No sign-up is required. Free and ad-free. Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Arabic and Simplified Chinese.
